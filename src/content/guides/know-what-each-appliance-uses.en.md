It is hard to cut electricity use without knowing where it goes. Measuring first tells you whether the aircon, a water heater, standby loads or something unexpected is the main part. It does not promise a saving. It tells you where to look.

## Smart plugs with energy reading

A smart plug sits between a wall socket and an appliance and reports how much it uses.

- **Good for:** a fridge, a TV and set-top box, a computer, a dehumidifier or a fan.
- **What it cannot do:** measure appliances that are wired in, such as many aircon units and water heaters.
- **Watch out for:** check the plug's rating against the appliance. A plug is not suitable for every heavy load.
- **Easy for renters,** because it needs no installation. See [smart home when you rent](/guides/smart-home-for-renters/).

## A distribution-board monitor

A monitor on the distribution board reads current on a circuit or on the whole home.

- **Good for:** wired-in appliances and whole-circuit totals, such as the aircon circuit.
- **What it needs:** work inside the distribution board. In Singapore this should be done by a licensed electrician.
- **What it cannot do:** tell you which appliance on a shared circuit caused a change, unless you compare it with other readings.

## Which should you start with?

- **Which plug-in appliance is wasting standby power?** Start with smart plugs.
- **How much does the aircon circuit use?** Start with a distribution-board monitor.
- **Renting, or no work allowed?** Start with smart plugs.
- **Planning a renovation?** Ask for a monitor to be planned in. See [planning before renovation](/guides/renovation-smart-home-prep/).

## What to do with the numbers

1. **Measure for a few weeks** so that you see weekdays, weekends and hot spells.
2. **Find the biggest user,** which is often the aircon. See [where the electricity goes](/guides/reduce-aircon-electricity-smart-home/).
3. **Change one thing at a time,** such as a schedule or a setpoint, and look at the reading again.
4. **Do not chase small loads** unless they run all day and all night.

## Be honest about the limits

- **Readings can differ** from your bill, because they are measured in a different place.
- **A reading is not a saving.** It only becomes one if you change a habit or a setting.
- **More data is not always more useful.** Three plugs in the right places beat thirty in the wrong ones.

## Common questions

### Do I need a monitor for the whole home?

Not to begin with. Plugs on a few appliances may tell you enough.

### Can a smart plug be used with an aircon?

Many aircon units are wired in or draw a high current. Do not put one on a plug that is not rated for it. Ask an electrician what suits your unit.

### Does it need the internet?

Readings can be stored locally. Remote viewing needs a connection. See [what still works without internet](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).

### Will it lower my bill?

Not by itself. It shows you what to change.

## A simple way to start

Put plugs on the fridge, the TV and one other appliance you are curious about, and read them for two weeks. Look at the biggest and the most surprising.

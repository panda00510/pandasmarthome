Singapore is humid all year round, so damp wardrobes, storerooms and bedrooms are an everyday problem rather than a seasonal one. A humidity sensor tells you when a space is staying damp, and an automation can respond by running a dehumidifier, a fan or the aircon's dry mode. What it cannot do is fix the cause, such as a leak or a room with no airflow.

## What a humidity sensor actually tells you

A small battery sensor reports humidity and temperature to your phone or your home controller. On its own that is just a number. Its value is in showing patterns:

- **Which spaces stay damp** after the rest of the home has dried out, such as a wardrobe against an outside wall or a storeroom with the door always closed.
- **When it happens:** overnight with windows shut, after laundry is dried indoors, or on days when nobody is home.
- **Whether something you changed helped,** such as leaving a door open or running a fan.

Our [leak, smoke and humidity sensors guide](/guides/water-smoke-humidity-sensors-singapore/) covers which sensors to choose and where to place them. This guide is about what to do with the readings.

## Automations worth setting up

### Alerts

The simplest automation is a notification when humidity in a space stays above a level you choose for a while. Set it to stay high for some time before alerting, so a brief spike from a shower or cooking does not send a message every day.

### Dehumidifier or fan on a smart plug

A plug-in dehumidifier or fan can be switched by a smart plug.

1. **Turn on** when humidity in the room rises above your chosen level.
2. **Turn off** when it drops below a lower level, so the appliance does not switch on and off constantly.
3. **Add limits:** only during certain hours, or only when nobody is sleeping in the room if the noise bothers you.

Check that the appliance resumes running when power returns. Some dehumidifiers come back on in standby and need a button press, which a smart plug cannot do. The manual or a quick test will tell you. Also check that the plug is rated for the appliance's power draw.

### Aircon dry mode

Many split aircons have a dry or dehumidify mode, and a smart infrared controller can usually send it in the same way as cool mode. What dry mode does, and how well it works, varies by brand and model, so read your aircon's manual. Our [smart aircon control guide](/guides/smart-aircon-control/) explains infrared and wired control, including why the app can sometimes show the wrong state.

A useful pattern is to run dry mode for a set period when a bedroom has stayed humid and nobody is using it, then switch off. If you are watching electricity use, see [where the electricity goes in a Singapore home](/guides/reduce-aircon-electricity-smart-home/).

### Wardrobes and storerooms

These are the spaces people check least. A sensor inside a wardrobe or storeroom can alert you when it stays damp, so you can open it up, move things away from the wall or run a dehumidifier nearby before items are affected.

## The limits

- **It does not fix leaks.** If a wall or ceiling stays damp, look for water: a pipe, the aircon drain, the window seal or a neighbour's bathroom. A leak sensor under the aircon drain helps here. A plumber or contractor fixes the cause.
- **It does not create airflow.** A room with no ventilation will keep collecting moisture. A fan or a door left open may help more than any automation.
- **Dry mode varies.** Not every aircon has it, and it behaves differently between models.
- **No health claims.** A sensor tells you about humidity, not about mould or air quality in the medical sense. If you are worried about mould and health, speak to a qualified professional.

## Planning it into a renovation

If you are renovating, think about ventilation and where a dehumidifier will plug in, especially in a storeroom or a built-in wardrobe. Leave a power point where it is needed. ## Common questions

### What humidity level should trigger the automation?

There is no single right number for every home. Start with the level the maker of your dehumidifier suggests, watch the readings for a while, then adjust it until the space stops feeling damp without the appliance running all the time.

### Can a smart home switch my aircon to dry mode?

Often, yes. A smart infrared controller can usually send dry mode if your remote has it. Whether dry mode exists and how it behaves depends on the aircon brand and model.

### Will a humidity sensor stop mould?

No. It tells you when a space is staying damp, so you can act early. Stopping mould depends on fixing the cause, such as a leak or poor airflow, and on what you do with the reading.

### Do these sensors work without the internet?

If they connect to a controller in your home, readings and automations keep running. Notifications to your phone need a connection. See [what still works without internet](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).

## A simple way to start

Put one humidity sensor in the wardrobe or storeroom that worries you most and watch it for a while before automating anything. The readings will tell you whether you need an alert, a dehumidifier or a fix to the room itself.

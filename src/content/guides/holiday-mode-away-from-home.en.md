A week away is when a smart home is most useful and when its limits show. It can keep a closed-up home from getting damp and tell you early when something goes wrong. It cannot walk in and fix a problem.

## Set the home for being empty

- **Aircon:** switch it off, or set it so it does not run for an empty home. A closed-up home in a humid climate can grow mould, so see the next point.
- **Humidity:** a dehumidifier on a humidity sensor, set to the levels you choose, can help in a wardrobe or a storeroom. See [leak, smoke and humidity sensors](/guides/water-smoke-humidity-sensors-singapore/).
- **Lights:** a few lights on a schedule can make the home look lived-in. It is a small touch, not a security measure.
- **Things to leave alone:** the fridge, the router, the controller and anything else that must stay on.

## Alerts that someone can act on

An alert only helps if a person reads it and can do something.

1. **Leak sensors** under the sink and by the washing machine, with a local siren too.
2. **A power or connection alert,** so you know when the controller or the router has gone offline. Notifications to a phone need internet. See [what still works without internet](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).
3. **A named person** who also receives the alerts and can go to the home. Tell them what each alert means.

## Doors and access

- **Check who has codes.** Remove temporary codes from visitors and contractors before you go. See [smart door locks](/guides/smart-door-lock-guide/).
- **Tell the helper or a neighbour** what to expect, in a language they are comfortable in. See [who gets access to what](/guides/smart-home-helpers-kids-guests-access/).
- **Do not announce your absence** more widely than you need to.

## What a smart home cannot do

- **It cannot stop a leak,** unless a shut-off valve is fitted. That is plumbing work.
- **It cannot confirm a person is safe,** and it should not be the only check.
- **It cannot act without power or internet,** so decide what you want to happen when either is lost.

## Cameras and privacy

Avoid adding a camera only for a week away. If you already have one, think about who can see its footage. See [smart home privacy](/guides/smart-home-privacy-local-vs-cloud/).

## Common questions

### Should I switch everything off?

Not everything. Keep the fridge, the router and the controller on. Switch off what you do not need, such as aircon and entertainment devices.

### Will lights on a timer deter a burglar?

It may make the home look occupied, but do not rely on it. Treat it as a small extra.

### What if the internet is down while I am away?

Local automations keep running, but alerts to your phone may not arrive. That is why a second person matters.

### Can I check from my phone?

Many systems allow it. Check how remote access works before you go, and do not leave it to the airport.

## A simple way to start

Before your next trip, run a short test: turn on the leak sensor alert, switch off the aircon and check the person who is meant to get the alert actually receives it.

Many homes use hot water for a short time each day, yet the heater may be on at other times. A schedule is an obvious thing to want. It is also the place where a casual smart home upgrade can go wrong, because a heater is a heavy electrical load.

## Know which kind you have

- **A storage heater** keeps a tank of water hot.
- **An instant heater** heats water as it flows and draws a high current while it runs.

The right approach depends on which one you have. If you are not sure, ask the installer, the maker's instructions or an electrician.

## What can be automated

- **A timer or schedule** that switches a storage heater on before the times your household showers, if the rating allows it.
- **A reminder or alert** if a heater has been on for an unusually long time.
- **A readout** of how much the heater uses. See [measure first](/guides/know-what-each-appliance-uses/).

## What you should not do

- **Do not put a heater on a plug-in smart plug** unless the plug is rated for that load. Many are not.
- **Do not change fixed wiring yourself.** Any new fixed connection or work in the isolator or board should be carried out by a licensed electrician.
- **Do not bypass** a thermostat, a cut-out or any safety part of the heater.
- **Do not make the schedule the only way** to turn the heater off.

## Keep a manual route

- **The wall isolator** should always work, with or without the smart system.
- **An override** should let anyone turn the heater on for a hot shower outside the schedule.
- **Tell the household,** including a helper and guests, how it works and how to turn it on by hand. See [who gets access to what](/guides/smart-home-helpers-kids-guests-access/).

## Will a schedule save energy?

It might, depending on how the heater works and how your household uses hot water. We cannot promise a number. Measure the heater for a few weeks and compare, and keep what you can show makes a difference.

## Think about the whole home

- **Aircon is usually a bigger share** of a home's use. See [where the electricity goes](/guides/reduce-aircon-electricity-smart-home/).
- **If you are renovating,** ask for the heater circuit to be planned with the electrician. See [planning before renovation](/guides/renovation-smart-home-prep/).

## Common questions

### Can I control my water heater from my phone?

Some setups allow it, but a heater is a heavy load. Ask a licensed electrician what is suitable for your heater before you add any control.

### Is a smart plug enough?

Only if it is rated for the load. Many are not, so check the rating and the heater's instructions.

### Should the heater stay on all day?

It depends on the type and your use. Measure before you decide.

### What if the system stops working?

The wall isolator and the heater's own controls should still work. Never depend on the smart system alone.

## A simple way to start

Find out which type you have and read its rating. Measure its use for a couple of weeks. Then talk to a licensed electrician about a suitable control, if the numbers justify one.

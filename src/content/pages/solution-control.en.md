A smart home that only works from one person's phone is a smart home that annoys everyone else. We design how people reach the system, so that grandparents, guests and helpers can all do the small things they need without installing anything.

## What we build

- **Custom phone and tablet dashboards** with the rooms and controls you actually use, not a screen of every device in the house.
- **A wall-mounted tablet or scene keypads** by the door and the bed, so common actions are one press away.
- **A simple mode** for anyone who just wants a button: lights, curtains, aircon, done.
- **Physical switches that keep working,** whatever happens to the app, the hub or the internet.
- **Voice assistants** where the devices support them. Useful, but we do not make anything depend on them.

## Designing for different people

1. **The person who likes to tinker** gets the full dashboard and the settings.
2. **The rest of the household** gets a handful of obvious buttons.
3. **Guests and helpers** get access that needs no app and no account, such as keypads and wall switches.

## Common questions

### What if someone does not want to use an app?

They do not have to. Physical switches work exactly as before, and wall keypads or a simple tablet cover scenes and common actions.

### Does everything stop working when the internet goes down?

No. Automations, wall switches, sensors and local dashboards run on the controller in your home. Only remote access and cloud-only devices pause until the connection returns.

### Can I use Apple Home, Google Home or Alexa as well?

Where the devices and platform support it, they can sit alongside your own dashboard. We tell you which parts depend on a vendor service before you rely on them. See [what Matter changes](/guides/what-is-matter/).

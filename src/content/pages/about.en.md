Panda Smart Home (Panda智能家居) designs and installs smart home systems for homes across Singapore: HDB flats, BTO and resale, condominiums and landed houses. We work in English and Chinese, and every page on this site exists in both.

## What we do

- **Smart homes.** Lighting, aircon, curtains, locks and cameras from different brands, running together on one local system. See [what we install](/solutions/) and [how a project runs](/how-it-works/).
- **Panda Signage.** Self-hosted digital signage and meeting room booking panels for offices, schools, clinics and shops. [About Panda Signage](/signage/).
- **Custom ESP32 AI hardware.** Prototypes and one-off builds, from new firmware on a device you already have to a prototype built from scratch. [Custom hardware](/custom-hardware/).
- **Custom software for small businesses.** Booking, inventory, WhatsApp bots and simple internal tools for shops and one-person businesses. [Custom software](/software/).

## How we work

- **Local first.** Automations run on a controller in your home, so switches and sensors keep responding when the internet drops. Remote access is optional and set up deliberately.
- **No single brand.** We choose devices on merit, not on which hub you already own, and connect them through open standards such as Matter, Zigbee and Thread on open-source software. There is no subscription between you and your own light switch.
- **In writing first.** Before any device is ordered, you get a written scope and device list, and the price is agreed. Signage, hardware and software work follows the same rule: scope and price in writing before work starts.
- **Handed over to you.** At the end you get the admin credentials, a written device inventory and a walkthrough. For custom hardware, the firmware, source code and documentation are yours too. You can run the system yourself.
- **Honest labels.** Stock photos on this site are marked as illustrative; they are not our installations or client projects. Devices that are not in stock are marked pre-order. Prices are indicative starting prices, and the amount you pay is agreed before anything is ordered.

## What we do not do

- We do not do fixed electrical work. New circuits, wiring in walls, switch boxes and the distribution board are for a licensed electrical worker. In a renovation we plan with your contractor's electrical worker rather than bringing a second crew.
- We do not replace your interior designer or main contractor. We turn what you want into a written list they can work from, and coordinate with them before the walls close. Our guide to [a smart home during an HDB renovation](/guides/smart-home-during-hdb-renovation/) explains who does what.
- We do not put a cloud service of our own between you and your home. Day-to-day control does not depend on a remote server.

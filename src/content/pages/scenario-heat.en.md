In Singapore, comfort is mostly an aircon, sun and humidity problem. This page brings together the pieces from our curtains, aircon and energy pages for one situation: the hot afternoons and humid days that every home here deals with all year.

## Keeping the sun out

- **Close before the room heats up.** Motorised curtains and blinds can follow sunrise, sunset and the west sun, so a west-facing room is shaded before it overheats. See [motorised curtains and blinds](/solutions/motorised-curtains/).
- **Shade without the dark.** Partial-open positions cut the glare and still keep some of the view.
- **No power at the window?** Battery motors and clip-on curtain robots suit windows where you cannot add a power point. Our [motorised curtains guide](/guides/motorised-curtains-guide/) covers the choices.

## Aircon only where someone is

- **Cool the room shortly before you arrive,** not hours earlier.
- **Switch off a room's aircon after no one has been there for a while,** using a presence or motion sensor. See [presence sensors: radar or motion](/guides/presence-sensor-mmwave-radar-vs-motion/).
- **Pause the aircon while a window or balcony door is open.**
- **Ease off at night:** raise the temperature a degree or two after bedtime, or switch off at a set hour.
- **Room by room:** each indoor unit of a multi-split system can be controlled separately. See [smart aircon control](/solutions/smart-aircon-control/).

## Damp corners

Singapore is humid all year, and wardrobes, storerooms and closed-up bedrooms tend to stay damp. A humidity sensor shows which spaces stay damp and when. An automation can alert you, run a dehumidifier or fan on a smart plug, or switch the aircon to dry mode where the model supports it. A sensor does not fix a leak or a room with no airflow, so find the cause first. See [humidity, mould and aircon dry mode](/guides/singapore-humidity-mould-aircon-dry-mode-automation/).

## Seeing where the electricity goes

The aircon is usually the biggest single use of electricity in a Singapore home, and it is hard to judge by feel. Measure first, then automate:

- **A smart plug with power measurement** for plug-in appliances such as a dehumidifier or a fan.
- **A meter on a circuit or for the whole home,** installed in the distribution board by a licensed electrician.

Automations help where waste comes from forgetting or an open window. They do not change what a unit uses while it runs, and we do not promise a particular saving. See [where the electricity goes in a Singapore home](/guides/reduce-aircon-electricity-smart-home/) and [energy monitoring](/solutions/energy-monitoring/).

## Common questions

### Will this lower my electricity bill?

It can help where waste comes from forgetting, an empty room or an open window. It does not change how much a unit uses while it is running, so the result depends on your home and habits. That is why we suggest measuring first.

### Do the curtains and aircon still work without the internet?

On a local system, yes. Curtain schedules and aircon automations run on the controller in your home. Only remote access and cloud-only devices pause during an outage. See [what still works without internet](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).

### Does my aircon have a dry mode the system can use?

Many split units do, and an infrared controller can usually send it in the same way as cool mode. What dry mode does varies by brand and model, so check your aircon's manual.

### Can I motorise the curtains I already have?

Often yes. Some products clip onto an existing rail or rod and push the curtain along. A track motor replaces the track, so it is a bigger job.

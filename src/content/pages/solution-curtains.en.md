In Singapore, curtains are as much about heat and glare as privacy. Motorised curtains and blinds open with the morning, close against the west sun and never need anyone to find a remote. The right choice depends on your windows, your power and how you want them to behave.

## What we work with

- **Motorised curtain tracks** that replace or sit in the track.
- **Roller and zip blinds** for windows and balconies.
- **Clip-on curtain robots** that push an existing curtain along its rail, useful where you cannot change the track.
- **Scheduling by sunrise, sunset and west sun,** so rooms are shaded before they overheat.
- **Partial-open positions** for glare control without losing the view.

## What to settle before you choose

1. **Track or roller.** It changes the look, the motor and the space you need.
2. **Wired or battery.** Wired needs a power point near the window. Battery needs charging, but suits places where you cannot add power.
3. **Which protocol.** It decides which other devices and apps the curtains work with. See [what Matter changes](/guides/what-is-matter/).
4. **Space for the motor.** A curtain box or pelmet needs enough depth. Tell your carpenter before it is built.

Our [motorised curtains guide](/guides/motorised-curtains-guide/) goes through the choices in detail.

## Common questions

### Can I motorise the curtains I already have?

Often yes. Some products clip onto an existing rail or rod and push the curtain along. A track motor replaces the track, so it is a bigger job.

### Do curtains need power at the window?

Wired motors do. Battery motors do not, though they need recharging. Decide during renovation if you can, because adding power later is harder.

### Will schedules still run without the internet?

On a local system, yes. Schedules run on the controller in your home. Only remote access and cloud-only devices pause during an outage.

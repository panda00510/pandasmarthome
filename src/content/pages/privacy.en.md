This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers smartpanda.sg in English and Chinese, and the 3D showroom at /showroom/. The site is run by Panda Smart Home ("we", "us"), and we aim to handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

## What the enquiry form sends

The form asks for your name and an email address or phone number. You can also pick a home type and what you would like to start with, and write a message. Along with these we send the address of the page you were on, the site language and the time you submitted. If your contact detail is an email address, it is also used as the reply-to address so we can answer you. A hidden anti-spam field that people never see is not sent.

Depending on how the site is set up, the form does one of two things:

- It sends your enquiry to a form-forwarding service, or to a forwarding service we run ourselves, which delivers it to our email or messaging tool. At the time of writing we use Web3Forms, which emails it to us. If we change this, we will update this page.
- If no forwarding service is set up, it opens your own email app with the enquiry filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

These services may store or process data outside Singapore.

## Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a plan or quote, and keep in touch about the work you asked us about. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your enquiry for advertising.

## WhatsApp, phone and email links

The WhatsApp, phone and email buttons open that app or service and take you off this site. Anything you send there is handled by that service under its own privacy policy, and reaches us through it. A WhatsApp button may fill in a short greeting, which you can edit before sending.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is a set of static pages, with no user accounts and no database of visitors. It is served by Cloudflare, with a copy on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, under their own policies.

## Visit statistics

On smartpanda.sg, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits. It does not use cookies.

We may also turn on Plausible Analytics or Google Analytics to count a few actions, such as a click on a WhatsApp button or whether a form submission succeeded. If enabled, these events carry only the page address, the site language and a fixed label for the action, never what you type into the form. Plausible does not use cookies. Google Analytics, if used, is set up with analytics and advertising storage denied, so it sets no cookies. Either service receives your IP address and browser details when its script loads. When neither is turned on, their scripts are not loaded at all.

## Cookies and local storage

The main site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser.

The 3D showroom remembers a few interface settings in your browser's local storage: the language you chose, whether the side panel is collapsed, whether you have seen the floor plan hint, and any hotspot positions you moved. They contain no personal data, stay on your device, and can be cleared in your browser settings.

## Fonts and other outside resources

The main site's fonts are served from this site. The 3D showroom loads two fonts from Google Fonts, so when you open it your browser sends a request to Google, which includes your IP address.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply to you and to support the work that follows. We do not keep them beyond that unless the law requires it.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to our using it. If you withdraw consent, we may no longer be able to help with your enquiry. To make a request, contact us through the [contact section](/#contact), or by email if an address is listed below, and say that it is about personal data. We will reply within a reasonable time.

## Children

This site is meant for adults planning work on a home or a business. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what the site does changes, for example a new form service or statistics tool. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. This version takes effect on 13 November 2026.

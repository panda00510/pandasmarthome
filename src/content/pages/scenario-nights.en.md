Night is when a smart home has to be at its most predictable. Nobody wants to open an app at midnight, and a light that switches off at the wrong moment is worse than no automation at all. This page brings together what our lighting, aircon, curtain, lock and control pages say about one situation: going to bed, the night itself and the morning after.

## Going to bed

- **A bedtime button by the bed,** on a scene keypad or a wall switch, so turning everything off does not need a phone. See [controls for the whole household](/solutions/home-control/).
- **Night mode.** The house switches to its night behaviour: the door is locked, and door and window sensors alert you if something opens. See [smart door locks and security](/solutions/smart-door-locks/).
- **The rest of the home goes dark,** so nobody walks round checking rooms before bed.

## A cool, comfortable room

- **Ease the aircon once you are asleep.** Raise the temperature a degree or two after bedtime, or switch off at a set hour. See [smart aircon control](/solutions/smart-aircon-control/).
- **Act on the real room.** A temperature and humidity sensor lets the automation respond to how the room actually is, not just to the clock.
- **Keep noisy things quiet.** If a dehumidifier or fan runs on an automation, limit it to hours when nobody is sleeping in that room. See [humidity, mould and aircon dry mode](/guides/singapore-humidity-mould-aircon-dry-mode-automation/).

## The walk in the dark

- **A night path:** a low light for the walk to the bathroom, instead of the full ceiling light. See [smart lighting](/solutions/smart-lighting/).
- **Lights that stay on while you are there.** A presence sensor keeps the bathroom light on during a longer visit, where a motion sensor alone might switch it off. See [presence sensors](/guides/presence-sensor-mmwave-radar-vs-motion/).
- **For older family members,** soft lights between the bed and the bathroom are one of the most useful changes. See [a smart home for elderly parents](/guides/smart-home-for-elderly-parents/).

## The morning

Motorised curtains can open with the morning, and close again against the west sun later in the day. See [motorised curtains and blinds](/solutions/motorised-curtains/).

## Common questions

### Will the lights go off while I am still in the bathroom?

They should not. A presence sensor notices someone still there, so the light stays on. A motion sensor alone can switch it off once its timer runs out.

### What if I do not want to use my phone at night?

You do not have to. A keypad or wall switch by the bed runs the bedtime scene, and the ordinary switches keep working.

### Will the night routine still work if the internet is down?

On a local system, yes. Scenes, schedules and sensors run on the controller in your home. See [what still works without internet](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).

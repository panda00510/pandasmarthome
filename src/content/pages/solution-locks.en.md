Security is where a smart home should be most dependable. We bring digital locks, door and window sensors and cameras together on one dashboard, so you can see the state of the home at a glance and get alerts that are worth reading.

## What we set up

- **Digital lock and gate status** in one view.
- **Door, window and motion sensors** with alerts that tell you what happened, not just that something did.
- **Away, home and night modes,** so the house behaves differently when you leave or go to bed.
- **Cameras with local recording** where the camera supports it, instead of depending on a vendor cloud.

## Choosing a lock

Retrofit or full replacement, fingerprint or PIN, and what happens when the battery dies are the questions that matter most. Our [smart door lock guide](/guides/smart-door-lock-guide/) covers the choices and backup plans.

Two things to settle early: how you get in if the battery is flat or the system fails, and who else needs access, including family and helpers.

## Common questions

### What happens to the lock if the internet goes down?

A local system keeps working. Automations, wall switches and sensors run on the controller in your home. Remote access and cloud-only devices pause until the connection returns. Read more in [what keeps working offline](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).

### Can I use my existing cameras?

Often yes. Many cameras use standards such as ONVIF or RTSP, which other systems can read. We check yours first. Our guide on [adding AI to existing CCTV](/guides/ai-upgrade-existing-cctv/) explains what is possible.

### Will cameras record locally?

Where the camera supports it, yes. Recording can stay on hardware in your home.

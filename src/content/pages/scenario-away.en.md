Most of what a smart home does while you are out is quiet: lights and aircon off, doors locked, and a message only when something actually needs you. This page brings together the pieces from our lighting, aircon, lock and sensor pages for one situation: the hours or days when nobody is home.

## Leaving the house

- **One action at the door.** An "away" button by the main door, or locking the digital lock, turns off the lights and the aircon. See [smart lighting](/solutions/smart-lighting/) and [smart aircon control](/solutions/smart-aircon-control/).
- **A room left on by mistake.** Presence or motion sensors switch off a room's aircon after no one has been there for a while. See [presence sensors](/guides/presence-sensor-mmwave-radar-vs-motion/).
- **Away mode.** The house behaves differently when everyone has left: door and window sensors send an alert when something opens. See [smart door locks and security](/solutions/smart-door-locks/).

## While you are out

- **Water leaks.** A leak sensor under the sink and behind the washing machine sends an alert before a small drip becomes a ceiling problem for the flat below.
- **Smoke.** A connected smoke sensor adds an alert to your phone, but it does not replace a proper home fire alarm device.
- **Doors and gates.** You can see whether the door is locked and the gate is closed, and get a message when one opens.
- **Cameras with local recording,** where the camera supports it, so footage stays on hardware in your home. We check your existing cameras first.
- **Decide who receives each alert, and who is the back-up,** in case you are on a plane or asleep in another time zone. Our [leak, smoke and humidity sensor guide](/guides/water-smoke-humidity-sensors-singapore/) covers this.

## A home closed up for days

A flat with the windows shut and the aircon off can get damp quickly in Singapore. A humidity sensor shows what is happening, and an automation can run a dehumidifier on a smart plug, or switch the aircon to dry mode where the model supports it. See [humidity, mould and aircon dry mode](/guides/singapore-humidity-mould-aircon-dry-mode-automation/).

## Someone dropping by

A helper, a family member or a cleaner may need to get in while you are away. Give each person their own door code, rather than sharing one or lending a key, and remove it when it is no longer needed. Our guide to [access for a helper, children and guests](/guides/smart-home-helpers-kids-guests-access/) covers codes, schedules and privacy.

## Coming back

The aircon can pre-cool the living room when you are nearly home, so you do not walk into a hot flat. The same arrival can turn the entrance light on.

## Common questions

### What happens while I am away if the internet goes down?

A local system keeps running: the lights, aircon schedules, sensors and the lock work on the controller in your home. What pauses is remote access, so alerts reach your phone again once the connection returns. See [what still works without internet](/guides/smart-home-without-internet/).

### Do I need cameras to keep an eye on the home?

No. Door, window, leak and smoke sensors tell you a lot without any camera. Cameras are an option, and where they support it, recording can stay in your home.

### Can a smoke sensor replace my fire alarm?

No. A connected smoke sensor adds alerts to your phone, but it does not replace a proper home fire alarm device.

### Can I let a helper in while I am travelling?

Yes, with a door code of their own on a digital lock that supports several codes. You can remove it later without changing anyone else's.

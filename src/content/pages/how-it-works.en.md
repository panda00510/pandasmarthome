You should know what happens after you get in touch. This is how we work, whether your home is a new flat in renovation or an older one that you already live in. The aim is a predictable process, with a written plan before any device is bought.

## 1. Consultation

- **You bring:** how your household lives, what annoys you today, and a list of the devices you already own, if you have one.
- **We deliver:** a plain answer on which of your existing devices can stay, before you spend anything more.
- **You can check:** no device is ordered until you have a written plan.

## 2. Survey and system design

- **You bring:** access for a site visit, or your floor plan.
- **We deliver:** a written device list, a network plan and the scope of work. In an older home this includes a wiring and network survey. See [what to check in an older home](/guides/check-older-home-before-smart/).
- **You can check:** the written scope, before anything is ordered.

## 3. Coordination

- **You bring:** your interior designer, contractor and electrician, if you are renovating.
- **We deliver:** what they need to know, before the walls close. See [what to prepare before renovation](/guides/renovation-smart-home-prep/).
- **You can check:** your own team keeps doing its job, and confirms the details with us.

## 4. Installation and configuration

- **You bring:** access to the home at the agreed times.
- **We deliver:** devices installed, the controller commissioned, dashboards built and automations tuned. Electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed personnel in accordance with Singapore requirements.
- **You can check:** each part against the written scope.

## 5. Handover and support

- **You bring:** whoever will run the system day to day.
- **We deliver:** a walkthrough, written documentation, the administrator credentials, and a support arrangement.
- **You can check:** you hold the credentials and the documentation, with no proprietary layer only we can service.

## Common questions

### How long does an installation take?

A focused single-area retrofit is often a day or two. A whole-home system runs alongside your renovation timeline. You get an indicative schedule with the written scope.

### How much will it cost?

Scope and pricing are quoted after a site assessment. We do not publish package prices that would need a dozen assumptions to be meaningful.

### Is there a monthly fee?

Not for the system itself. It is open source and runs on hardware in your home. Optional extras, such as a cloud service you keep or a support plan with us, are always stated separately.

### Can we talk in Chinese?

Yes. English or Chinese, whichever the household prefers.

## AI edge box for your existing CCTV

A small box on your own network that reads the video streams from cameras you already have and adds analytics. The video is processed on site rather than sent to a cloud service.

- Designed for ONVIF or RTSP cameras you already own.
- People counting for shops, offices and entrances.
- Alerts for unusual events, such as movement in an area at an unexpected hour.
- Counts and alerts shown on the same local dashboard.

**Worth knowing:** which cameras work depends on their stream format, lighting and mounting angle. We check yours before promising anything. Read more in [adding AI to your existing CCTV](/guides/ai-upgrade-existing-cctv/).

## Fall detection without a camera

A radar sensor that notices a fall and raises an alert without capturing images or sound. It is meant for bathrooms and bedrooms, where a camera is not appropriate.

- No video or audio recorded.
- Alerts to family phones or your dashboard.
- Planned: flag when a room has been still for an unusually long time.

**Worth knowing:** radar still senses movement and presence, and one sensor covers a limited area, so larger rooms may need several. It is a safety aid, not a medical device or a replacement for care. We will test it in real homes before quoting accuracy. Read more in [fall detection without a camera](/guides/radar-fall-detection/).

## Privacy comes with both

Counting people and monitoring spaces involves personal data. In Singapore, organisations that use CCTV have duties under the PDPA, including telling people why footage is collected. We plan for this with you.
